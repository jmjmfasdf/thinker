BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        floor > Immovable hidden=True img=oryx/white.png autotiling=True
        carcass > Immovable img=oryx/brown.png shrinkfactor=0.6
        goat > stype=avatar
            angry  > Chaser cooldown=8 img=oryx/gold.png
            scared > Fleeing cooldown=3 img=oryx/red.png
        avatar > MovingAvatar img=oryx/darkblue.png
        wall > Immovable img=oryx/darkgray.png

    InteractionSet
        goat   wall goat   > stepBack
        avatar wall    > stepBack
        avatar  angry  > killSprite scoreChange=-1
        carcass scared > killSprite
        scared avatar  > transformTo stype=carcass scoreChange=1
        scared carcass > transformTo stype=angry

    LevelMapping
        0 > scared floor
        . > floor
        A > floor avatar
        w > floor wall

    TerminationSet
        SpriteCounter stype=scared win=True
        SpriteCounter stype=avatar win=False
