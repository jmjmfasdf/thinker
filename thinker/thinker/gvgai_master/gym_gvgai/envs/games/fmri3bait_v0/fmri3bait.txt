BasicGame key_handler=Pulse square_size=50
    SpriteSet
        floor > Immovable hidden=True img=oryx/white.png
        hole > Immovable img=oryx/blue.png
        avatar > MovingAvatar
            nokey > img=oryx/darkblue.png frameRate=8
            withkey > img=oryx/darkblue.png frameRate=8
        mushroom > Immovable img=oryx/red.png shrinkfactor=0.6
        key > Resource img=oryx/orange.png shrinkfactor=0.7 limit=1
        goal > Immovable img=oryx/green.png
        box > Passive img=oryx/brown.png shrinkfactor=0.8
        wall > Immovable img=oryx/darkgray.png autotiling=True

    LevelMapping
        A > nokey 
        0 > hole floor
        1 > box floor
        k > key floor
        g > goal floor
        m > mushroom floor
        . > floor

    InteractionSet
        avatar wall > stepBack
        avatar hole > killSprite
        box avatar  > bounceForward
        box wall box mushroom > undoAll
        box hole > killBoth
        nokey key > transformTo stype=withkey
        key avatar > killSprite
        nokey goal    > stepBack
        goal withkey > killSprite scoreChange=10
        mushroom avatar > killSprite scoreChange=1

    TerminationSet
        SpriteCounter stype=goal limit=0 win=True 
        SpriteCounter stype=avatar limit=0 win=False
